# HG4D

Building software that serves humanity.

We design and develop systems grounded in quality, ethics, and human benefit. From prediction markets and financial transparency tools to AI-assisted decision making, we build for problems that matter.

## Our Mission

To create software that upholds the highest standards of quality and ethical practice, making tools that genuinely improve human decision-making, transparency, and trust in systems that affect real lives.

## Our Work

We build tools for complex problems that require both technical rigor and ethical clarity.

## Our Values

Quality, ethics, and human benefit aren't aspirational—they shape every decision we make.

### Quality First

We prioritize correctness, clarity, and robustness over speed. Our code is built for people who will read it, maintain it, and depend on it. Testing, documentation, and simplicity aren't nice-to-haves—they're requirements.

### Ethical by Design

We consider the impact of our systems on the people who use them and those who are affected by them. Transparency, fairness, and accountability are embedded in our architecture, not added later.

### Human-Centered

Technology should make human judgment better, not replace it. We build tools that augment human capability, preserve agency, and make it easier for people to understand and trust the systems affecting their lives.

### Radical Honesty

We communicate clearly about what our systems do, what they don't do, and where they can fail. We acknowledge limitations and unknowns rather than paper over them with confidence.

### Open When Possible

We believe in transparency and shared knowledge. We publish open-source tools, share what we learn, and participate in communities working toward better, more ethical technology.

### Sustainability

We build for the long term. This means sustainable business practices, maintainable code, and choosing quality over vanity metrics. We're in this for the compounding effect of doing good work consistently.

## How We Work

### Write less code better

We choose clarity and simplicity over complexity. A smaller codebase that's easy to understand is better than a large one that's clever.

### Tests are specifications

Our tests document what the system should do. They're written first, and they're kept as readable as the code itself.

### Monolithic when sensible

We avoid premature distribution. Single applications with clear layers are easier to reason about, operate, and test than complex distributed systems.

### Iterate openly

We share our thinking as we build. Code review, documentation, and communication aren't overhead—they're where quality happens.
